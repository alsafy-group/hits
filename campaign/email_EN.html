HITS MADE SIMPLE

Dear colleagues,

Remember HITS? It has been on your phone all along. With HITS you can request leave and permissions, track your attendance and fingerprint records and, if you lead a team, approve your team’s requests, anytime and from anywhere.

To make every task easier, we’ve put together HITS Made Simple: a guide that walks you through each step with screenshots from the app itself, and stays with you as a reference whenever you need it.

Open the guide now and save it to your phone, right next to HITS.

Save the guide on your phone:
iPhone: open the link in Safari (if it opens inside your mail app, choose "Open in Safari" first), tap Share (or ⋯, then Share), then "Add to Home Screen".
Android: open the link in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu at the top, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
